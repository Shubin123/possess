        Teach your own poses

        Name a pose, hold it in front of the camera, and record a few seconds. Two poses with about 100 samples each are enough to train.

          Save and load

          Samples are kept in this browser's local storage between visits. Exported files are plain JSON: feature vectors for the samples, weights for the model.

          Pose estimation by MediaPipe Pose Landmarker, downloaded once and cached locally.
